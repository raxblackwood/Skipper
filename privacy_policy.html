Official Policy

Privacy Policy for Skipper

Effective Date: October 17, 2026

Developer: raxblackwood

Summary: Skipper operates 100% offline and on-device. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal messages, keystrokes, personal identity data, or device credentials to any server.

1. Introduction

Skipper ("we", "our", or "the App") is an automation utility designed to streamline partner skipping and match management on supported chat applications (AnonChat, Chatous). We are committed to transparency and respecting your digital privacy.

2. Accessibility Service API Usage & Disclosure

Skipper explicitly uses Android's AccessibilityService API to deliver its core automated functionality. In compliance with Google Play's User Data policy:

  • Purpose of Use: The Accessibility Service is used solely to inspect screen status (such as partner disconnect indicators or profile bio headers) and perform emulated taps on user-specified action buttons (e.g., "New partner", "SKIP") to reduce repetitive manual tapping.
  • Data Accessed: The App temporarily reads UI node text strictly within the designated target applications (io.anonchat, com.chatous.chatous) to identify match states.
  • Data NOT Collected: Skipper NEVER logs, monitors, collects, or records keystrokes, passwords, private chat messages, financial credentials, or microphone/camera input.
  • No Network Transmission: The Accessibility Service operates entirely offline. None of the screen data or node information is uploaded to external servers or shared with third parties.

3. Information We Collect

Skipper does not require account creation, registration, or personal login information. Local preferences (such as click delay, enabled apps, and trigger modes) are stored strictly on your local device using Android SharedPreferences.

4. Data Security

Because all automation and text analysis happen locally in your device's memory, your data never leaves your smartphone. We do not maintain any cloud databases or external telemetry infrastructure.

5. User Control & Permissions Revocation

You maintain full control over the App's permissions at all times. You can enable or disable the Accessibility Service whenever you wish via Phone Settings → Accessibility → Skipper.

6. Children's Privacy

Skipper does not knowingly collect or solicit information from anyone under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated Effective Date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via GitHub: https://github.com/raxblackwood/Skipper.
